KEY#1) SPEAK THE BORN AGAIN MESSAGE 
“How then shall they call on Him in whom they have not believed? And how shall they believe in 
Him of whom they have not heard? And how shall they hear without a preacher? And how shall 
they preach unless they are sent? As it is written: “How beautiful are the feet of those who preach 
the gospel of peace, Who bring glad tidings of good things!”” 
Romans 10:14-15 NKJV 
"Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God" (John 3:3). 
“You believe that there is one God. You do well. Even the demons believe—and tremble!” 
James 2:19 NKJV 
“Therefore, having been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ, 
through whom also we have access by faith into this grace in which we stand, and rejoice in hope 
of the glory of God.” 
Romans 5:1-2 NKJV 
KEY #2) PREACHING MUST CONFRONT SIN AND POINT PEOPLE TO THE SAVIOR 
“Preach the word! Be ready in season and out of season. Convince, rebuke, exhort, with all 
longsuffering and teaching.” 
II Timothy 4:2 NKJV

“King Agrippa, do you believe the prophets? I know that you do believe.” Then Agrippa said to 
Paul, “You almost persuade me to become a Christian.”” 
Acts 26:27-28 NKJV 
KEY #3) DON’T BE ‘THE ALMOST CHRISTIAN’ 
"Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the 
will of my Father which is in heaven" (Matthew 7:21). 
“Examine yourselves as to whether you are in the faith. Test yourselves. Do you not know 
yourselves, that Jesus Christ is in you?—unless indeed you are disqualified.” 
II Corinthians 13:5 NKJV 
KEY #4) REVIVAL MUST PRODUCE MORE THAN EMOTION — IT MUST PRODUCE A CHANGED 
LIFE 
"Ye shall know them by their fruits. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles?" 
Matthew 7:16 
"Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all 
things are become new." 
2 Corinthians 5:17 
A true revival changes the way people live. 
It changes how husbands treat their wives. 
It changes how wives treat their husbands. 
It changes how parents raise their children. 
It changes how young people make decisions. 
It changes how people conduct business. 
It changes how we treat the poor. 
It changes how we respond to those who disagree with us. 
It changes our attitudes toward sin. 
It changes our commitment to prayer and Scripture 
“But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, 
gentleness, self-control. Against such there is no law.” 
Galatians 5:22-23 NKJV 
A genuine response to a spiritual awakening should make us more like Jesus. 
It should make us more loving, more compassionate, more truthful, more forgiving, and more committed to 
righteousness. 
The evidence of a genuine revival is not merely how loudly people shout in church, but how faithfully they live 
for Jesus when they leave church! 
KEY #5) THERE MUST BE CONVERGENCE 
Convergence is the act or state of coming together from different directions to meet at a common 
point, limit, or similarity. 
“There is one body and one Spirit, just as you were called in one hope of your calling; one Lord, 
one faith, one baptism; one God and Father of all, who is above all, and through all, and in you all.” 
Ephesians 4:4-6 NKJV 
“that they all may be one, as You, Father, are in Me, and I in You; that they also may be one in Us, 
that the world may believe that You sent Me.” 
John 17:21 NKJV 
The devil wants to divide the church over secondary matters while the world waits to hear the primary 
message of salvation! 
A SPIRITUAL AWAKENING CAN UNITE PEOPLE WHO HAVE BEEN DIVIDED 
“with all lowliness and gentleness, with longsuffering, bearing with one another in love, 
endeavoring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.” 
Ephesians 4:2-3 NKJV 
“I tell you, no; but unless you repent you will all likewise perish.”” 
Luke 13:5 NKJV 
KEY #6) BELIEVE IN THE POWER OF THE GOSPEL 
A revival that begins in the heart can eventually influence a family, a community, a church, and even a nation. 
CONCLUSION: WHAT AMERICA AND THE WORLD NEEDS MOST IS ANOTHER SPIRITUAL 
AWAKENING 
We should pray and follow these keys for another spiritual awakening. 
We need revival in our pulpits. 
We need revival in our pews. 
We need revival in our homes. 
We need revival among our young people. 
We need revival in our communities. 
And we need revival in our own hearts. 
The greatest contribution Christians can make to America is not simply to argue about what is wrong with the 
nation, but to live and proclaim the gospel that can change the hearts of its people. 
“and from Jesus Christ, the faithful witness, the firstborn from the dead, and the ruler over the 
kings of the earth. To Him who loved us and washed us from our sins in His own blood, and has 
made us kings and priests to His God and Father, to Him be glory and dominion forever and ever. 
Amen.” 
Revelation 1:5-6 NKJV
